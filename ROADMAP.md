# Product Roadmap - Relaxio Spa Enterprise CRM

## Version 1.0.0 (General Availability - Current)
- ✅ **Core CRM & Check-in Desk**: Real-time customer registration, duplicate mobile number checks, and visit history.
- ✅ **Automated Invoicing & GST**: Sequential `RLX-2026-XXXX` invoices, thermal receipt & A4 printing.
- ✅ **Realtime Dashboard**: Live revenue analytics, therapist leaderboards, agent commissions, and payment breakdown.
- ✅ **Role-Based Access Control**: Multi-tier permissions for Super Admin, Admin, and Staff Desk.
- ✅ **Audit Logging & Security**: Immutable activity trail, rate limiting, RLS policies, and production security headers.
- ✅ **Data Export Engine**: High-speed CSV, Excel (.xlsx), and PDF reports restricted to Super Admin.

---

## Version 1.1.0 (Q3 2026 - Planned)
- 🤖 **Gemini AI Assistant**:
  - Automated peak-hour appointment scheduling advice.
  - Revenue forecasting & therapist performance insights.
  - Natural language queries for customer data.
- 💬 **WhatsApp Business Cloud API**:
  - Automated PDF invoice links sent directly to customer WhatsApp.
  - Appointment confirmation and 2-hour pre-visit reminders.
  - Post-treatment review & feedback collection loops.
- 📅 **Advanced Calendar & Appointment Scheduling**:
  - Drag-and-drop therapist schedule calendar.
  - Online booking widget for spa website.
- 💳 **Membership & Loyalty Points Engine**:
  - Tiered membership programs (Silver, Gold, Platinum, VIP Royal).
  - Prepaid wallet packages & automatic point redemptions.
- 🏢 **Multi-Branch / Multi-Location Support**:
  - Enterprise view aggregating metrics across multiple branches.
  - Inter-branch therapist allocation & central inventory sync.

---

## Version 1.2.0 (Q4 2026 - Future Outlook)
- 📦 **Inventory & Consumables Tracking**: Real-time tracking of massage oils, skincare products, and linen laundry loops.
- 📱 **Native Mobile Apps (iOS / Android)**: Dedicated React Native build for handheld POS devices.
- 🌐 **Offline First Syncing**: PWA offline queue for reception desks during internet outages.
